## Why six and not sixty

There's already a good library. [microsoft.github.io/skills](https://microsoft.github.io/skills) has 175+ skills with one-click install, and it's a better place to *get* skills than anything this site would build.

These six exist to be **read**. Each one is annotated with why each section is the way it is, and each one loads into the [Skill Forge](/skillup/lab/forge.html) with a button so you can pull it apart.

## What to look for

<div class="stack-rows">
<div class="stack-row"><b>Descriptions</b><p>Every one names concrete nouns somebody would type, and every one has a "use when" clause. Compare them and you'll see the pattern.</p></div>
<div class="stack-row"><b>Boundaries</b><p>Five of the six say what they're <em>not</em> for. That one line prevents more misfires than anything else in the file.</p></div>
<div class="stack-row"><b>Output formats</b><p>All six show the shape rather than describing it. That's not a coincidence.</p></div>
<div class="stack-row"><b>Length</b><p>Between 35 and 70 lines. None of them needed to be longer, and the two that were, in their first drafts, got split.</p></div>
</div>
