## The shift

At three skills, every problem is a writing problem. At forty, most problems are design problems.

<div class="table-scroll">

| | With 3 skills | With 40 skills |
|---|---|---|
| Matching | Almost always right | Overlap and competition are the default failure |
| Token cost | Irrelevant | ~1,600 tokens of descriptions on every single turn |
| Quality | You remember what each does | You have no idea if the one you wrote in March still works |
| Ownership | You | Somebody who left |
| Changes | Edit and move on | Something else breaks and you find out in a month |

</div>
