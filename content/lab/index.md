## A suggested order

<div class="stack-rows">
<div class="stack-row"><b>1</b><p>Run your idea through <a href="/skillup/lab/decide.html">Should this be a skill?</a> first. A meaningful share of proposed skills turn out to be instructions, knowledge, or a one-off prompt, and finding that out takes ninety seconds.</p></div>
<div class="stack-row"><b>2</b><p>No idea yet? <a href="/skillup/lab/ideas.html">Generate three</a> for your role, pick the one that made you nod, and hit Start in the Forge.</p></div>
<div class="stack-row"><b>3</b><p>Write it in the <a href="/skillup/lab/forge.html">Forge</a> until the score stops moving, then take the file to whichever platform you're shipping to.</p></div>
</div>

<div class="note brass">
<p class="eyebrow">What the Forge is and isn't</p>
<p>It's a linter. It checks structure, language, and the things that reliably break skills. It can't tell you whether your procedure is correct, whether your business rules make sense, or whether the skill will fire against a real model. Only <a href="/skillup/400-advanced/evals.html">running it against a test set</a> tells you that.</p>
</div>
