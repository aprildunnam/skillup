# Vinyl condition grading

Grading is an important part of the resale process at the shop and helps
ensure that customers know what they are buying. Our shop follows
industry-standard practices for condition assessment, which promotes
consistency and trust across all of our inventory categories and helps
maintain our reputation with collectors.

When grading, staff should generally consider both the disc and the
sleeve, and it is usually preferable to note them separately where
possible. The Goldmine scale should typically be used. Graders should
try to be accurate and should avoid overgrading, which can lead to
returns and dissatisfaction.

It is worth remembering that the sleeve and the disc can be in quite
different conditions, and that this is worth mentioning. Scratches are
significant, particularly deeper ones. Ring wear on the sleeve is also
something to bear in mind, as is the state of the seams.

Generally speaking the response should mention the condition of the
record in a way that would be useful to somebody pricing it.
