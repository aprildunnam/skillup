Lab

The lab

Everything here runs in your browser. No sign in, no API key, no backend, nothing leaves the page.

Skill ForgeWrite a SKILL.md, watch a live scorecard tell you which line is going to break it. Ships with six good skills and three deliberately broken ones. Should this be a skill?Seven questions, six possible verdicts. Often the answer is no, which is the most useful thing it can tell you. Skill idea generatorPick a role and a platform, get three skills worth building, each one loadable straight into the Forge.

A suggested order

1

Run your idea through Should this be a skill? first. A meaningful share of proposed skills turn out to be instructions, knowledge, or a one-off prompt, and finding that out takes ninety seconds.

2

No idea yet? Generate three for your role, pick the one that made you nod, and hit Start in the Forge.

3

Write it in the Forge until the score stops moving, then take the file to whichever platform you're shipping to.

What the Forge is and isn't

It's a linter. It checks structure, language, and the things that reliably break skills. It can't tell you whether your procedure is correct, whether your business rules make sense, or whether the skill will fire against a real model. Only running it against a test set tells you that.
